How I work

Four steps,
every time.

No matter the size of the project, the process is the same — because it works.

Understand

I ask a lot of questions up front — often ones you haven't been asked. The goal is to solve the real problem, not the one in the brief.

Sketch

Rough wireframes, a rough plan, a rough estimate. Nothing precious — just enough to argue about before writing code.

Build

Small commits, working software, and regular check-ins. You see progress every week, not every month.

Refine

The last 20% is where products become good. I sweat the details, tune the feel, and make sure it's genuinely nice to use.
